Team Greggs Reaction-Time Experiment

Within this experiment we're measuring reaction-time to various colour changes to determine if colour impacts reaction speed.

To the right you'll start the experiment by clicking on the large square, and click again each time the colour changes (a total of 20 times).

Once you've completed all 20 cycles you'll be able to view and export your average results per colour.

Click anywhere within the square to begin the Reaction Time Test.
